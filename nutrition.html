Nutrition

Simple nutrition guidance for busy students.

🥗 Balanced Meals

Include vegetables, fruits, grains and protein sources in your meals.

💧 Hydration

Drink water regularly throughout your college and study routine.

🍎 Healthy Snacks

Choose fruits, nuts and other nutritious snacks between meals.

🍽️ Meal Routine

Try to maintain regular meals instead of frequently skipping them.
